How it works

Three steps. Mostly one button.

  1. 1

    Drop it in

    Drag videos or a whole folder onto the window, paste a link, or press Ctrl+V anywhere. Mooxr guesses the title and artist for you.

  2. 2

    Press Moox

    Files convert one at a time in the background. Cancel one, retry a failed one, keep adding more while it works.

  3. 3

    Play it in Spotify

    Each MP3 lands in your Spotify folder and shows up under Your Library → Local Files by itself.

Chrome extension

Moox it from the page you’re on.

What it decides for you

The fiddly bits, handled.

Leave the boxes empty and Mooxr works it out. Type over anything you want to change.

Title and artist, filled in

From the link’s details, the file’s own tags, or a name like Artist - Title. The grey text shows its guess before you start.

Cover art that fits

The video’s thumbnail or a frame from it, cropped to a square JPEG up to 3000×3000, which is what Spotify wants. Or pick your own.

Titles without the clutter

(Official Video), [HD] and (Lyrics) come off. (feat. X) and (Live) stay.

320 kbps MP3

44.1 kHz stereo, saved as Artist - Title.mp3 in an album called Mooxed so everything groups together.

Mooxed files library

Every track you’ve made, with its cover. Search, sort, fix a title, swap the art; the Spotify copy updates to match.

No repeats, no half files

Already mooxed something? You’re told when you add it. Stop mid-way and nothing half-written is left behind.

One-time Spotify setup

Spotify doesn’t let other apps hand it songs; it watches folders. Point it at Mooxr’s folder once and every track appears by itself.

  1. In Spotify, open Settings → Your Library
  2. Turn on Show Local Files
  3. Press Add a source and choose Music\Spotify
  4. Find your tracks under Your Library → Local Files

Questions

Good to know

What does Mooxr need to run?

A Windows PC. Mooxr.exe has everything packed inside. The first time you open it, it offers to fetch FFmpeg (the tool that does the converting, about 115 MB) and checks the download before using it.

My antivirus asked about Mooxr.exe. Is that normal?

Some antivirus tools are wary of new, unsigned programs and ask before running them. The first launch also takes a few seconds while Windows unpacks the file.

Which music apps can it send to?

Spotify and Apple Music pick tracks up from a folder automatically. YouTube Music, Deezer and SoundCloud only take uploads on their websites, so Mooxr opens the upload page and shows you the file to drag in. Tidal and Amazon Music don’t accept your own files.

Why did a renamed track drop out of my playlists?

Spotify recognises a local file by its title, artist, album and length. Change the title or artist and it’s a new song to Spotify, so add it again. Mooxr warns you before you save. A new cover is fine.

Do I need the extension?

No. The app works on its own with files and pasted links. The extension just saves you copying the link across.

Is there a command line?

Yes: python -m mooxr "My Band - First Song.mp4". Add --title, --artist or --out as needed. It can be used from Python code too.
